Office Assistant (m/f)

Lumi Logistics GmbH · Prizren, Kosovo · Full time

About the role

Lumi Logistics is a freight company with a growing team in Prizren. We are looking for an office assistant who keeps the front office running and supports our dispatch team.

Your tasks

  • Answer phone calls and emails from customers and drivers
  • Prepare delivery documents and keep the archive in order
  • Plan meetings and keep the shared calendar up to date
  • Enter shipment data in Excel and our internal system

What we expect

  • Good command of MS Office, especially Excel and Word
  • Fluent Albanian and good English; German is an advantage
  • Well organised and calm under pressure
  • Completed secondary school; a degree in economics is a plus

We offer

A permanent contract, paid training and a friendly team. Salary 550–650 EUR net.

Send your CV to jobs@lumi-logistics.example.org by 15 December.
